# Design notes and assumptions

## Approved appearance

Aalind selected the recovered purple robot reference images for this rebuild. The rejected older STL assembly was not used. This is a new Python/CadQuery construction, developed with Ava/Hermes assistance.

## Scaling convention

STEP and STL exports use millimetres. The portable GLB is converted to standard metres and +Y up; the alternate VTK glTF retains CAD millimetres/+Z up. Head width is assumed to be 190 mm solely to establish a convenient editable scale. Other dimensions are image-proportion estimates, not measured physical hardware. The overall bounds are generated by the code and reported in `exports/validation.json` rather than manually maintained here.

The source exposes a parameter dictionary plus named profile coordinates and local construction values. It is editable procedural CAD, not a fully constrained product configurator: changing a major dimension requires reviewing dependent inner shells, openings and adjoining geometry. Always rerun all checks after edits.

## Reference features retained

- Closed monitor-like indigo head, rounded corners and broad teal display.
- Circular head-side grilles with distinct outer rings.
- Two outward-leaning flat antenna tabs with dark inset faces.
- Low sensor puck on the roof with small front-facing apertures.
- Rounded body that widens below the shoulders and rounds inward near its base.
- Two curved dark front-body trim bands, circular chest detail and offset indicator.
- Side wheel pair with substantial tires and swept-spoke hubs.
- Small twin-wheel support underneath, informed by the detached caster video frame.
- Short rounded side appendages.

## Interpretation, not source recovery

The source renders contain perspective, some ambiguous features and earlier cutaways. The model approximates smooth surfaces with analytic primitives and spline lofts. It does not recover exact original NURBS, original dimensions, hidden mechanisms or the original engineering assembly.

- Rear shell is deliberately simple where the source does not establish its details.
- Antenna and sensor modules are cosmetic proxies.
- Body/head split seams are design suggestions, not tested fastening interfaces.
- Real motor/battery/PCB geometry is not guessed from the old rejected model.
- No motors or electronic internals are included, avoiding a false claim of compatible packaging.

## Verification scope

- All named component BREP solids checked with OpenCascade validity and positive volume.
- Exact pairwise intersections tested after bounding-box broad-phase pruning. Threshold is 0.5 mm³; exceptions are reported rather than treated as clear.
- Every STL checked for watertightness, winding consistency, finite vertices and positive volume.
- Exported STEP re-imported; solid count and volume compared with pre-export inventory.
- GLB re-imported; geometry count compared with the inventory.
- Preview images are rendered from the exported geometry, not generated reference art.

These checks demonstrate coherent exported geometry, not fabrication readiness, successful assembly, electronics fit, movement or regulatory/event eligibility.
